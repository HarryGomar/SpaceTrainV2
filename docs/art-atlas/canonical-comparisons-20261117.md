Three are promoted from the existing catalogue; two are new source-bound institutional reproductions. This is public curation for every participant, not a rule fitted to a particular person's responses.

## Reproductions and source limits

Nighthawks comes from the [Art Institute record](https://www.artic.edu/artworks/111628/nighthawks), [API](https://api.artic.edu/api/v1/artworks/111628), and its linked IIIF image. The source served an 843 × 461 JPEG. The current API's non-public-domain flag and null copyright notice are preserved in the rights explanation; metadata CC0 is not treated as an image licence.

Guernica comes from the [Reina Sofía record](https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/collections/artwork/guernica-0/) and its directly linked 1440 × 652 WebP. Its [current rights notice](https://www.museoreinasofia.es/en/node/37878) identifies © Sucesión Pablo Picasso, VEGAP, Madrid, 2026.

Both actual local compositions were inspected. Original bytes, native dimensions, hashes, source links and credit are retained without crop, resampling or upscaling. Preview and larger viewing use the same native asset; fine unresolved surface qualities stay unknown. All sixteen operational qualities were considered independently. Physically impossible imagery is not automatically assigned to Guernica just because its forms are distorted.

Existing catalogue records and captured answers remain unchanged. Already shown older questions retain their original instruments. A fresh run receives the new first-block curation; a saved run receives applicable analysis and presentation improvements without inventing responses to unseen paintings.

## Interpretation and presentation

Conditional favourite/dislike comparisons now require agreement on other reviewed qualities before receiving an explanatory card. Unknown controls supply no agreement. Supporting pairs are selected for comparability, and the view explains the shared annotation and remaining differences. These are editorial safeguards, not calibrated confidence or causal isolation.

The reading retains a whole-profile introduction, with centred paintings, consistent restrained colour and emphasis tied to generated patterns. The optional refinement invitation is a compact action bar. Dense comparison maps have question filtering and a selected-painting focus rather than requiring the visitor to untangle every edge at once.

The reading catalogue contains families that may combine, remain unresolved or be unavailable under older instruments. They are not fixed personality types. Implemented and planned families are explicitly distinguished on the Profiles page.

The Paintings page now divides every later 50-round pool into small browsing sets with titles and comparison notes. Source-bound quality reviews supply contrasts where available; reviewed visual descriptors provide a fallback, and missing evidence receives a neutral description. These gallery arrangements do not replace the quiz's adaptive comparisons or become preference evidence. The first block retains its declared curation, and answered comparisons retain their captured works and instructions.

Collection additions and reset are available through the compact Manage saved menu rather than a separate explanatory section. The opening animation has no painting-pool toolbar.
