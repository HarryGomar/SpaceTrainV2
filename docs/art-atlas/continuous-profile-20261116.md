# Personal appeal, levels and gaps

The new instrument is `personal-appeal-continuous-v1`. Its captured question, hint and three anchors must match its definition. It uses every integer position from 0 to 100. Fifty is neutral. A painting above neutral supplies a positive report; one below neutral supplies a negative report. The older 65/35 bands remain available for stronger examples and retain their original meaning in older overall instruments.

The profile keeps three different observations separate:

- **Level:** paintings can all appeal, all fail to appeal, or span neutral. A local winner is not necessarily liked.
- **Difference:** a wider gap records a larger reported difference in personal appeal.
- **Grouping:** close positions describe similar appeal in that question. They do not establish the same psychological reason for liking the works.

## Quality and axis comparisons

The new channel is `personal-appeal`. It never pools with the older overall channel or the frozen order-only channel.

For a descriptor contrast, each pair supplies its signed gap divided by 100, capped at a 75-point difference. These contributions are averaged over all eligible contrasts in the group, including exact ties. One group therefore contributes at most 0.75 in either direction. This prevents the six pairs of a quartet from acting like six independent demonstrations. The cap is a reporting design choice, not an estimate of psychological precision.

A directional group must have at least twice as much weighted support as opposition, with a net average gap of five points. Every nonzero recorded difference is retained. A close group can therefore remain unresolved for a broad quality even though its painting order is known. A recurring reading still needs three distinct groups, six disjoint artwork examples and three preferred-work artists. Later and repeated responses are checks, rather than additional discovery examples.

An overall heading additionally needs three actual above-neutral examples across artists and groups. A relative tendency among paintings rated below neutral remains visible as a comparison. It cannot supply a positive taste identity.

Across-set quality associations use one mean per group on each reviewed side. This separate level analysis allows homogeneous groups to contribute. Offered paintings, subject, artist and correlated qualities remain possible explanations. Neither analysis produces confidence percentages, population percentiles or causal claims.

## Direct spacing and returns

`portrait.responseSpacing` retains every discovery group’s actual values, mean, range, adjacent gaps, neutral-side membership and clusters spanning at most ten points. Its short observations describe tight groups above or below neutral and large separations. These are direct response descriptions and are ineligible as profile names.

Continuous repeats retain per-work deltas, mean-level change and original signed pair gaps. A changed level or compressed range is reported as a change on return even when the order holds. It is not a reliability judgment. Repeats preserve both original questions and source IDs.

## Compatibility and source binding

- Frozen v12 order-only answers still use order and exact ties. Their coordinates do not become liking levels or gap strength.
- Captured v11 overall questions retain their original 15-point comparison rule and 65/35 example bands.
- Current explicit favourite and dislike declarations keep priority, actual page exposure and source-identity guards. Non-selection never becomes dislike.
- Continuous closing checks validate their own frozen weighted training snapshots and report actual test gaps. They do not refit earlier predictions after answers change.
- Source-bound artwork reviews retain unknown values. Different reproductions, malformed identities and unsupported protocol markers fail closed.

The thresholds in this implementation are provisional product reporting rules. They are not a validated psychometric instrument.

## Context and arrangements

The separate `reported-appreciation-change-v1` question asks directly whether a sourced note adds or takes away appreciation. Its midpoint means no change. “Much more” remains a positive change when an earlier personal-appeal rating was already 100. No after-rating is reconstructed. Optional earlier evidence requires the captured original question, complete source answer, reproduction identity and source ID. An uncertain response remains unknown. Repeated reports retain their history but counts use distinct artworks.

Source-reviewed museum and sorting relationships can attach to an already qualified taste quality when they share actual supporting artworks. This adds a separate arrangement context and its exceptions. It never changes preference support counts, weights, qualification or independence claims. A bundled taste connection labels a grouping link as only one component when the arrangement supports only one reviewed quality.
