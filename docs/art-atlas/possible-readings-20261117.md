# Possible Art Atlas readings

Art Atlas combines evidence-based reading families; it does not assign a fixed personality type. There are 51 catalogued families: 41 for current instruments, 8 retained for older instruments, and 2 proposed but not generated. A family can be absent or unresolved when its inputs or evidence are insufficient.

The live catalogue is at https://harrygomar.com/art-atlas/profiles.html. Examples are illustrative, never assertions about a participant.

A complete profile can combine visual connections, response distinctions, contextual reports, arrangement decisions and exploration routes. Repeated paintings remain related evidence; a collection of supporting cards is not independent replication.
