# Adaptive taste in Art Atlas

17 November 2026. Baseline and production were both `6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d`, verified against the remote branch, the production deployment record, and the public files. The runtime changes in this report are in the separate adaptive-taste worktree. The first-150 curation audit, proposal, and inventory were untracked in the main checkout. Its 17 additions, nine promotions and 26 deferrals were proposals. No candidate became playable through the proposal alone.

## Diagnosis

The quiz could learn which paintings stood out, but the scheduled quality follow-up began with aggregate counts of earlier support. It mostly compared works the participant had already rated. It could nominate a lightly matched pair, and a failed search often showed part of a recent quartet again. Its answer populated a standalone observation. The ranking of explanations, the suggested comparison in the profile, and the optional question selected by the host each came from a different calculation. This was the biggest obstacle to a quiz that learns during the session. See the baseline versions of [explanation-followups.mjs](https://github.com/HarryGomar/SpaceTrainV2/blob/6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d/public/art-atlas/explanation-followups.mjs), [profile-intelligence.mjs](https://github.com/HarryGomar/SpaceTrainV2/blob/6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d/public/art-atlas/profile-intelligence.mjs), and [profile-refinement.mjs](https://github.com/HarryGomar/SpaceTrainV2/blob/6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d/public/art-atlas/profile-refinement.mjs).

The stronger existing parts are worth keeping. The neutral midpoint on fresh lines makes high, low and near-equal groups distinguishable. Historical marked order-only questions have a separate path. An artwork set supplies at most one weighted direction per quality. Source-bound reviews require an explicit absence. The better boundary finder already needs eight other known qualities, at most three differences, and agreement on at least three quarters of the known controls. It correctly refuses to call Las Meninas versus Tamayo's Still Life with Foot a lighting contrast. Their reviews differ on six of fourteen other jointly known qualities. The previous general quality selector had a weaker screen. [Baseline taste-axes.mjs](https://github.com/HarryGomar/SpaceTrainV2/blob/6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d/public/art-atlas/taste-axes.mjs) · [Baseline taste-boundaries.mjs](https://github.com/HarryGomar/SpaceTrainV2/blob/6b24f27d92962872d039b6616e0de5166d9e205d/public/art-atlas/taste-boundaries.mjs)

A local, in-memory replay of the most recent response export restored 50 shown questions and 50 valid answers. In eleven slots, the visible type, wording or works differed from the schedule. The archived order-only run also had differences. The recent export contained no optional attempts, though it could produce an invitation. Removing one supporting group, omitting a contributing artist, or withholding one source review changed the principal explanation list in that replay. These are audit facts about one run. Private answers, profile wording, files and screenshots were kept out of this worktree.

There was also a literal reading error. When two appeal patterns qualified and there were no declared favourites, the hero could say “Your favourites connect through…” and then surface zero examples. An opposing comparison could be illustrated with the higher-rated work and describe it as a lower-rated exception. Both paths now use the actual channel and evidence role. Admiration still has its own reading. Recommendations based on personal appeal no longer take their lead from admiration.

Up to one of those works can appear before its ordinary reservation in a landscape slot. This makes a graphic field, a painterly seascape or a layered river view available through an extra comparison inside the same 50 interactions. ## Which model to build

| Direction | What it earns | Main cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Global quality predictor | A common baseline and predictions across different paintings. Can find recurring associations outside the obvious favourite set. | A small, selectively shown sample and correlated editorial features can make a useful predictor give a misleading explanation. One average can hide separate reasons for liking Bosch and a spare abstraction. |
| Local explanations with cross-artist checks | Starts from an actual strong liking, dislike or declaration. Picks a comparison whose possible answers change the explanation. Can support several routes and show where each stops. | A strong response can deserve investigation long before it deserves a conclusion. Many plausible distinctions have few adequate comparators. |
| Separate artist and context models | Can distinguish an artist's treatment from one work, and model note-, room- or task-specific responses. | Needs repeated, comparable observations. A separate model for every artist or channel is thin in 50 questions. |

Use local investigations as the primary adaptive method. Keep the broader quality model as a source of candidate transfer checks and qualified recurring readings. Artist, medium, subject, recognition, image and task are constraints or rivals. Context and arrangements are decisions with their own instructions. A directly measured dissociation, such as high admiration with low appeal, may be a clearer finding than any image-based explanation.

### What the research warrants

- Art liking varies substantially between people. Iigaya and colleagues predicted held-out ratings from image statistics and human-coded features using a fixed-weight individual model. Their seven intensively sampled participants each rated 1,001 paintings. This makes a global model a credible baseline for prediction. It does little to validate a causal account from a selective 50-question run. Specker and colleagues also found much higher agreement for a few judgments than for many broader aesthetic effects. Editorial reviews supply distinctions to test. [Vessel et al.](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29936343/) · [Iigaya et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8494016/) · [Specker et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0232083)
- Van Paasschen and colleagues found increased liking and beauty after prior experimental exposure in their museum study. Leder found weaker familiarity-liking associations when inspection time or authenticity framing changed. Park and colleagues observed familiarity effects that differed by image complexity and stimulus type. Inference for Art Atlas is to track self-reported prior recognition and in-session learning item by item. One cued attribution cannot identify an expertise trait. [Van Paasschen, Bacci and Melcher](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0134241) · [Leder](https://doi.org/10.2190/5TAE-E5CV-XJAL-3885) · [Park et al.](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2021.694927)
- In Leder and colleagues' title experiment, elaborative titles improved understanding of abstract paintings after 90 seconds without raising appreciation. Swami found that relevant context improved appreciation and understanding for selected abstract Picasso paintings, with different results for representational paintings. A randomized six-work museum trial found no systematic gain. These conditions support asking for a direct change report and retaining the note's identity. The quiz's question measures the person's attribution of change. [Leder et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.actpsy.2005.08.005) · [Swami](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030965) · [Museum trial](https://edoc.unibas.ch/entities/publication/d8c0ea9a-c5c9-4af4-b15d-95688d3f312d)
- Turner and Silvia observed interest in art without requiring pleasantness. Brieber and colleagues measured viewing time with eye tracking and found that the relationship between ambiguity and looking differed by setting. Asking about appeal, attention and return separately can earn a meaningful dissociation. It does not make the reported quantities behavioural measurements. [Turner and Silvia](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17144758/) · [Brieber et al.](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0099019)
- Parducci and Wedell changed the distribution and presentation of physical-size stimuli and observed shifts in category ratings. Goldstone and colleagues showed that comparison sets can change the dimensions people use for perceived similarity. These are different tasks from art appeal, so the design inference is modest. Keep the reported anchors and gaps, and treat changes across groups with care. Kriegeskorte and Mur explicitly instructed participants to arrange by dissimilarity and modelled multiple arrangements. Museum placement and unlabelled sorting do not meet those instructions. [Parducci and Wedell](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2946806/) · [Goldstone et al.](https://doi.org/10.3758/BF03201115) · [Kriegeskorte and Mur](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00245)
- Guo and Sanner's value-of-information method requires a probabilistic preference and answer model. Art Atlas uses explicit eligibility, candidate ranking and a fallback. Kossen and colleagues demonstrate how adaptive evaluation sampling can bias a performance estimate. Freeze predictions before responses and distinguish an adaptively selected comparison from the broad sample. Measure any future predictive accuracy on protected, held-out work. [Guo and Sanner](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v9/guo10b.html) · [Kossen et al.](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/kossen21a.html)

## What the quiz selects

1. **Within 50.** Keep 18 ordinary comparison panels and the existing 50 slots. Local checks can use the slots at 15 and 23. A transfer check can use 18. Round 27 samples landscape breadth. The group already sorted can earn a companion question at 32. Round 50 can act on the earlier favourite and dislike declarations. Each slot has a broad appeal fallback for unavailable or weak contrasts. Keep one artist attribution at 17, linked to actual earlier answers, and the independently measured response maps, sourced notes and museum. Make a useful first profile even when the participant declines extras.
2. **Through 100.** Keep discovery dominant. In the full routed catalogue the ordinary planner targets sixteen exploration panels, one exact repeat and one challenge. The extra in-block checks still replace scheduled tasks. Earlier directions must survive works by artists outside the original observations. If the direction has only one viable explanation or two rivals agree on the available choices, spend the slot on wider coverage.
3. **Through 150.** Seek transfer outside the earlier artist, subject and handling, including the third landscape exploration panel. A second local follow-up can carry forward its surviving explanation and put it beside a different rival. Another check of the same comparison has little value.
4. **Later.** Continue in blocks of fifty up to 300. Preserve the independent pool, repeats and checks. Later released work expands the range of eligible partners. The participant can accept up to ten optional questions after the first completed block, one at a time, with a return to the reading after each. Pending questions resume as shown. An optional question records where it occurred, including after 100 or later. Extra movement matching and chronology have educational value, but the fresh base no longer spends slots on them. A future opt-in knowledge activity should remain separate unless its answer changes a specific familiarity comparison.

### Selection objective and fallback

The first question about a local response is whether a contrast is worth spending a slot on. A strong level means at least 25 points from neutral. A gap of at least 12 to the nearest answer or 35 across neutral can lift a response out of its group. These are provisional selection rules. They distinguish a 98/97 cluster from 98/10 while preserving both responses. Explicit favourites and dislikes can nominate a work directly. Repeat agreement helps priority. A clear same-work reversal blocks the original directional nomination until there is a renewed direct declaration. Keep scores from each occurrence.

A local question starts with two plausible reviewed properties of the exact anchor. It looks for two works whose known present/absent statuses predict opposite directions for these alternatives. They come from different artists than the anchor and each other. An explicit favourite and dislike that share many reviewed controls can provide the boundary. One target must be new to anchored rating, and the target pair must be new as an anchored comparison. A transfer check starts from separate discovery sets. One explanation must already recur and a rival needs at least two supporting groups. Both targets must be unrated. They may have appeared in a different task.

The pair must have eight jointly reviewed controls, differ on at most three, and agree on at least three quarters of the known controls. The local targets also need some reviewed overlap with the anchor. Among feasible pairs the selector favours unrated work, less repetition, better control match, similar known subject or medium, and less imbalance in observed familiarity or label exposure. Candidate count does not boost an explanation's standing. The search ranks a bounded shortlist for each side. Adjacent scheduled panels constrain opportunity. No catalogue prestige enters the new pair score. This is an editorial comparison heuristic.

Up to one local target per block can come forward from a later pool if the pair earns it. Normal regional stage and reproduction eligibility still apply. The landscape slot can use one later landscape from the reviewed list. An explicit saved or placed work can be the anchor of an optional first rating even when reserved later. These are recorded supplemental appearances. The planner still owns ordinary release. When a pair fails the screen, a base slot samples distinct artists and favours gaps in coverage. It may use already rated paintings in a new grouping if eligible unrated choices have run out. The optional selector ends the offer when no question would add a useful distinction.

The host keeps the optional offer, actual question and profile next step on the same proposal. After local checks, the optional order considers an actual museum fit question, a cross-artist transfer comparison, a sorting companion check, an anchored map recheck and a first rating for a saved, kept or placed work. An ordinary general question does not fill an unused optional place.

## How a reading changes

The investigation record contains the anchor IDs, earlier observation or declaration, relevant source rounds and captured training positions, two directional alternatives, their opposed predictions, review identity and control coverage, earlier exposure, previously rated target IDs, and the later exact answer. The former and current responses survive editing. A later revision to a source answer makes that check historical for the current reading.

| New evidence | Profile treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Isolated strong response or explicit choice | Keep it as an anchor for exploration. A preference for an artist or movement needs more than one work. |
| One clear local contrast | Describe the named works and which possibility gained support. State the unresolved visual difference or the next useful check. Keep the conclusion local. |
| Second usable pair on the same anchor | Carry over the surviving explanation where possible. Agreement adds a specific example. Opposed outcomes qualify or replace the earlier local account instead of becoming two isolated claims. |
| Exact tie, small gap, both below neutral on a liking prediction, or both above on a dislike prediction | Keep the alternatives open or narrow the claim to the original painting. A gap under ten does not select a rival. A large gap between two disliked paintings does not demonstrate transfer of liking. |
| Repeated work, overlapping quartet, selected check | Retain the original record and its later relation. The comparison cannot add another independent discovery vote. An unrated work inside a partially overlapping group keeps its level even if its group cannot support an independent pair. |
| Broad cross-artist transfer against a qualified claim | Mark the implicated broad appeal explanation mixed and withdraw its headline eligibility. One agreeing transfer adds a concrete check while leaving the overall cause open. Other channels and independently supported routes still have their own readings. |
| Genuine counterexample or missing source | Show a counterexample after the strongest supporting works with its actual role. Unknown review values cannot manufacture matching or contrary evidence. A room omission may lead to a fit question and stays open about motive. |

For two principal patterns, use the actual evidence channel in the introduction and foreground positive and explicitly selected works. Keep weaker sections useful by describing the next distinction. When ratings are strong enough for a local reading but broader patterns split, a short explanation around named paintings is more honest than a generic label. Distinguish a concrete next artwork recommendation from a general collection link. A recommendation based on visual similarity names the shared feature and the evidence it came from.

### Arrangements and participant explanations

Start from a room or a nearby pair they actually placed, with the recorded prompt and exposure. Ask whether the retained work and a presented omission each fit the same group. Hold the offered space and purpose fixed in the instruction. For a second check, find one outsider sharing colour, material detail, period, story clues, atmosphere, rhythm, relationship or another plausible property without its rival counterpart. Current sorting checks can do this for two separable reviewed visual qualities. For a museum fit check, the participant can report that both belong, neither belongs, or they are unsure. That keeps redundant work and space open even when neither choice matches the earlier appeal order.

Optional self-explanation belongs after a recorded choice. “What mattered most when you placed this work here?” allows the person to name something the annotations miss. “Which difference mattered most to you?” can be asked after a pair, with room for another reason and uncertainty. An answer proposes a follow-up and sits alongside the original choices. It earns a place only when a feasible next question could change a conclusion. Neither prompt is required by the current implementation.

## Implementation and verification boundary

1. The integrated slice uses existing responses and reviews to nominate strong anchors, screen pairs, preserve coverage, distinguish selected checks, update readings and offer specific optional comparisons. Existing layout and interactions carry the new wording and comparisons.
2. Before expanding the visual vocabulary, complete paired source reviews for concrete disputes such as a single photographic face, print treatment, colour field, cultural reference and serial repetition. Warhol, Basquiat and Haring remain conditional on separately approved curation and the actual reproduced images. The current 16 qualities cover several relevant shapes and surfaces. They cannot resolve familiarity, cultural association or conceptual interest by themselves.
3. For a new measurement, start with one question that can actually separate the remaining alternatives. Item-specific prior recognition, a directly stated reason for a room placement or an additional written note can each add evidence in a narrow case. Higher precision about familiarity, causes of changes after notes, or curatorial motives needs prospectively captured questions and comparison designs. Exposure inferred from the catalogue cannot substitute.
4. At each reading checkpoint compare the leading thread with one influential set removed, with one contributing artist removed, and with that work's uncertain review withheld. Compare capped gap weighting with equal directional group votes. Log whether the direction, qualification or rank changes. For a before/after profile, first replay the same frozen questions under both readers, then add new responses. This distinguishes a changed interpretation from a changed stimulus mix. Prospectively selected checks in the branch record their target IDs and predictions before the answer. A fixed broad or randomly withheld set is needed to estimate transfer accuracy, separately from the selected checks.

The numerical gates and budgets are product hypotheses. The calibration question is whether the new questions are perceptibly more useful and the readings better held up by independent responses. A local replay or synthetic probe cannot establish that. See [worked cases](adaptive-taste-worked-cases-20261117.md) for the expected readings, limits and continued questions.

### Verification on this branch

A synthetic trajectory rated every available fresh appeal line through six blocks and skipped the other tasks. Each block retained 18 ordinary ratings groups and 50 base interactions. There was one artist task in the first block and none in later blocks. The three landscape slots brought forward Tanner's *The Seine*, Segantini's *Pine Tree* and Bannister's *Palmer River*. They were compared with a range of open and enclosed treatments, including Friedrich's *Moonrise over the Sea* and Homer's *Early Morning After a Storm at Sea*. The test generated both local investigations and transfer checks, then returned to exploration in later transfer slots after useful rivals ran out.

For the first profile on that trajectory, the leading natural-setting and open-expanse directions held when a supporting quartet was withheld, when groups containing Hokusai were omitted, and when the source review of *The Great Wave* was withheld. All three perturbations added material detail as a lower-ranked candidate. The natural-setting direction also held with one directional vote per group in place of the capped gaps. The leading directions survived these perturbations. The full list of explanations did not. A secondary thread can depend on the choice of groups or a single review even when the headline looks stable.

In a separate held-out test, two rival qualities both had earlier support in independent quartets. The selector froze a pair of previously unrated paintings and their opposed predictions. The next rating gave one 93 and the other 10. The losing explanation lost headline eligibility. The new pair added no discovery group. Other focused checks cover equal positions, closely spaced all-high and all-low ratings, source mismatch, unknown controls, response edits, non-selection, the historical order-only instrument, optional resume and ordering after round 100, and a source note adding appreciation at a prior 100.

A completed synthetic sort put *The Kiss* beside Mondrian's *Composition with Red, Yellow, and Blue*. The actual round-32 binder reused those anchors and offered Rothko's *Orange and Yellow* and Friedrich's *Wanderer above the Sea of Fog*. The companion choice could distinguish geometric structure from precise rendering for that group. The final local replay restored all 50 saved questions and answers, retained their original plan, catalogue and five archives, and rendered all nine profile categories. The optional action and its category linked to the same valid comparison with one previously unrated work.
