# Canonical additions, 16 November 2026

The catalogue adds 24 individually reviewed works, 17 Western classics and seven works in a Mexican cultural or practice context. The selection adds dense scenes, quiet relationships, selective lighting, symbolic figuration and fantastical worlds. These are curatorial priorities, not claims about a visitor's preferences or population recognition.

At the append-only import, all 463 previous catalogue objects and their image bytes were preserved. The resulting catalogue has 487 works, 487 source-bound sixteen-attribute reviews, and 282 strict literal action/setting reviews. A further 202 draft action/setting records remain pending and three partial mural views remain gallery-only. A subsequent source audit corrected Stańczyk's current object URL, as explained below; its image bytes, ID, title and descriptors stayed fixed.

## Image and annotation limits

The 48 final JPEGs consist of one preview and one large derivative per work. Previews have a maximum edge of 800 px, large derivatives 3200 px. Derivatives preserve the downloaded composition and aspect ratio and never upscale. Large files total 39,765,325 bytes.

Three source-limited works retain their real dimensions: Creation of the Birds, 1568 by 1316; The Two Fridas, 1196 by 1200; The Escape, 1568 by 1931. The media resolver reports these limits. Holbein's complete panel is intentionally very narrow. Adam is explicitly a border-trimmed named central scene, not the entire Sistine ceiling. Dualidad includes surrounding photographed museum architecture, which is excluded from the painted-setting judgement.

Each exact local reproduction was inspected on contact sheets. All sixteen operational criteria were considered separately. Fine paint handling that could not be resolved at the review scale stays unknown. Source identity binds the institution URL, preview path and actual preview SHA256; the inspected large asset has its own SHA256 and dimensions. Present and absent visual descriptions do not establish a psychological cause for liking.

The seven provisional numerical descriptions were individually judged for these new works. Only action and setting receive the strict `literal-action-setting-v2` marker. A static corpse remains low in action; dramatic mood does not create a depicted setting; museum walls around a mural are not painted landscape. Documented iconographic and technical notes remain separate cited context.

Three researched alternatives were not imported because a suitable verified reproduction was not obtained: Varo's Embroidering the Earth's Mantle and Celestial Pabulum, and Kahlo's Broken Column. There are no placeholder records for them. The Escape retains the source-reported 1961 date and the 1961–1962 alternative rather than hiding the discrepancy.

## Verification and reproduction

`tools/art-atlas-canonical-manifest-20261116.json` is the committed public ID inventory. Tests read the published catalogue, review sidecars, checked-in manual observations and real asset files, with no dependency on ignored import staging. The canonical regression covers identity, rights distinctions, native sizes, hashes, all sixteen explicit statuses, source binding and strict literal semantics. The original 463-work literal-audit fixture remains separately testable.

The research/import/finalize/merge tools retain cached source evidence in ignored local QA folders. The merge checks every candidate before writing and asserts that all existing catalogue objects remain unchanged. Repeating the merge with the same prepared input is safe; different prepared content under an existing ID is rejected.
