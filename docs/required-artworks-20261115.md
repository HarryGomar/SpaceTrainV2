# Required collection additions

The candidate package contains19 works and38 verified local JPEGs. Sixteen candidates can enter ordinary study selection. Three partial cycle views remain gallery research only. The live catalogue was not edited by this import.

`artifacts/art-atlas/required-expansion-candidates.json` exports `.works`. The separate `required-expansion-attribute-reviews.json` exports `.reviews` and `.currentAssetReferences` using the existing sixteen-attribute contract. Every attribute is present, absent or unknown and has a reason. Reviews identify the assistant as reviewer and bind the exact preview source URL, path and SHA256. They are editorial descriptions, not participant answers or psychological measurements.

The38 derivatives total28.74MB. Previews have a maximum800px edge. Large images retain actual pixels up to3200px, with no upscaling. The final source and derivative hashes, dimensions, byte counts, rights scope and transformations are recorded per work. Original downloads and visual contact sheets remain in ignored `qa-output/required-20261115/`.
