About Art Atlas.

Art Atlas compares your choices with your separate responses to the same works. Personal appeal, admiration, scene clarity, possible interpretations, tension and the wish to return remain distinct. Readings show their source choices and exceptions. The study has not been psychometrically validated.

Curatorial exercises record what you place together, separate or leave out. Room membership and deliberate spacing can provide evidence. Automatic wall spacing cannot. Artist matching and date questions describe art recognition, separately from preference.

Start with 50 rounds and continue in further groups of 50. After the first 50, you may be offered up to 10 optional comparisons before viewing your profile. Saved answers retain their original paintings, questions and scales. Your session and collection are stored in this browser. Responses are not sent to a server. Downloads let you keep a copy.

The main collection focuses on classics and art from the Americas, including Mexico. Additional world collections remain available separately. The opening uses distinct artworks from the main collection. The collection is historical and has substantial gaps in recent art and several painting traditions. It cannot represent all art or measure how unusual your taste is.

Museum and artist suggestions explain a connection to a work you chose. Collection links do not guarantee that an artwork is currently on view. Sources and image rights are linked with each work.
