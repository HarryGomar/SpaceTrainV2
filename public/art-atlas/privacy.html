Privacy and terms

Privacy

Your quiz progress, saved paintings and galleries are saved in this browser as you use Art Atlas. You can take the quiz, see your profile and build galleries on this device without signing in.

If you choose Continue with Google, Google provides your email address and basic profile to Supabase, which manages sign-in and stores your account. Art Atlas uses your account ID to control access to your uploaded copies and displays your email so you can see which account is open.

Choosing Upload copy sends a copy of the selected data to Supabase for private storage and restoration on another device. A quiz copy includes your answers, the questions and artwork references behind them, and any earlier runs kept with your quiz. A gallery copy includes your rooms and painting placements.

Uploaded copies stay in your account until you delete them or remove the account. To remove a copy, open Manage copies on the account page and choose Delete uploaded copy. Clearing Art Atlas site data in your browser removes device saves and recovery copies.

For privacy requests or account deletion, email harrygomardawson@gmail.com.

Terms

Art Atlas lets you compare paintings, build galleries and explore a profile based on your answers. These readings describe patterns in the responses you gave to the works you saw.

Use an account you control when you choose to upload a copy. For a separate copy you can keep as a file, use the download controls in Art Atlas.

Artwork credits and collection sources are linked from individual works. Permission to reuse a reproduction depends on the rights listed by its source.
